    How a shop is filed.

    1. The shopEvery card charge carries the shop’s name and a code.
    2. Its codeThe bank files the shop under a merchant category code (MCC).
    3. Its categoryBonuses, caps and exclusions are written against these categories.
    4. Every card’s termsEach card’s own terms are run on the amount, at that shop.

    Card designs are drawn by VerFi from each bank’s published artwork; figures are on a typical month.

    Not financial advice. Rates are read from each bank’s published terms; check the bank’s current terms before applying.
